🌍 Broadcast to All Users

By default, only paid users (with priority slots) and admins can broadcast to all users. Enable this to allow everyone to broadcast to all users.

💡 How it works:
• Users watch ads to earn points (default: 10 points per ad)
• When they have enough points (default: 30), they can create a broadcast
• Creating a broadcast deducts the required points
• Admins can broadcast without needing points
